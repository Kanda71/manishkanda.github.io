Justifying Reasons

This is the introductory post. I have been trying to justify to myself why I made this site, so I came up with three reasons (reasons that will keep me working on this site).

First reason. Photos. We—cameras more than we. Efforts to find continuity perished—busy posing, me in pictures, framing backstories, long before unsmiling smiles, conscious, too conscious. Long before vanity started. (See gallery section.)

Second reason. Master's Project. My Lab, Logs. Meetings with my professors. New tools I learn. New methods. Papers that fascinate me, and, in all, I, somewhere. (See physics section.)

Third reason. Reading. I read. I read fiction mostly. Characters. Places. Countries. The way it is told, the abstractness it has. I took courses outside my domain. Humanities courses. Exploration of the Himalayan culture and its people. Treks I did. New places I explored. I will put everything here.

Welcome to the stream of my consciousness.
