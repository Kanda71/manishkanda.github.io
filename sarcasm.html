Justifying Reasons

Alright, I’ve uploaded the photos and will upload more. Yes, we’re supposed to click millions of pictures wherever we go. First, let’s take photos with everyone, and then fuck all that — click your own pictures from every angle, at every different place. I truly believe that we’re just too lazy to use Photoshop and change backgrounds, and honestly, it’s a bit difficult to learn it that well. So instead, we’ll spend some cash and go somewhere with a good background for photos, because in the end, all that matters is you — only you. Just blur the fuckin’ world and show yourself with a slightly blurred background to that blurred world. They’re just waiting for the moment you upload the photo. Their whole life is stuck to those moments.
